# Yonsei University 音乐本科跳过说明（2026-08-13）

跳过，不建包：Yonsei 当前 2027 Spring International Students Application Guide 的外国人本科招生单位未列 College of Music；官网政策另明确自 2025 Spring 起，国际学生招生不再招收 College of Music。依据：[2027 Spring International Students Application Guide](https://admission.yonsei.ac.kr/seoul/admission/html/counsel/dataView.asp?BBS_NO=3503&s_data=2027)；[Yonsei 官方国际招生政策说明](https://admission.yonsei.ac.kr/seoul/upload/guide/202306271739017W94QC.PDF)。
